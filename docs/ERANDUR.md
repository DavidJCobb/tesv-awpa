
# Erandur

**BLUF:** Erandur is not a resident of Dawnstar per se, but several townspeople know him by name and have enough investment in his actions to remember when and where they see him. It makes sense for him to be included as part of AWPA.

Erandur was recruited to Vaermina worship as a child, and at some point during his youth, he engaged in that worship in Cyrodiil. He must have been brought to The Pale not long after, since he says he grew up there, and since he describes the hold as the place he's spent most of his life in.

He states that he briefly studied at the Bards College in Solitude, but left when his affiliation with Vaermina was discovered. He isn't explicit about whether this occurred before or after he abandoned Vaermina worship; however, I think that the very latest it could've happened was after he fled Nightcaller Temple but before he turned to worshipping Mara.

Erandur was away from the Pale for years. Erandur states that he's never set foot in Whiterun, on the College of Winterhold's grounds, nor in Riften's Temple of Mara. His description of Riften suggests that he knows the city only by reputation and not firsthand experience. His description of Markarth also makes reference to the city's reputation, but the wording is less "I've obviously never been here," and he also expresses awareness of rumors about neighboring mining towns, which suggests at least a general familiarity with the Reach overall.

It's implied that he helped an injured miner (Sylgja) at or near Shor's Stone, and that this occurred specifically during his return to Dawnstar, so he would've had to had this encounter with her without actually passing by Riften. There are a few paths he could've taken if he started outside of Skyrim:

* The most direct path would've been to cross from Morrowind into Skyrim via the road at Skyrim's southeast corner, and then circumnavigate Riften either south-to-north (such that traveling toward Dawnstar would bring him past Shor's Stone) or east-to-west (circling around Lake Honrich to go to Shor's Stone). The latter route makes sense if he was specifically avoiding either Riften (due to its reputation) or the Temple of Mara (feeling he needed to redeem himself before he'd have the right to visit it).

* Less direct paths have him crossing from Cyrodiil into Skyrim, south-to-north, via either the road southwest of Falkreath or the road south of Helgen. However, he would have had to take a very indirect path from there, heading east through the Pale Pass into Ivarstead, and then circling southeast along the Treva River, walking along Lake Honrich's north edge, and then turning north at the main road to pass through Shor's Stone. It'd be strange for him to take such a roundabout path instead of just crossing the plains of Whiterun Hold directly to the Pale.

If he began his return to Dawnstar from inside of Skyrim, then the situation becomes murkier. If he's never been to Riften, but he passed through Shor's Stone, then he would necessarily have had to take an indirect path to Dawnstar. If for example he began his return from Windhelm, then he would've had to head south through Shor's Stone, before either backing up to the north and heading west, or continuing further south around Riften and walking the roads north of Lake Honrich to head west. These are paths that only make sense if he wanted to actively avoid traveling the more direct, but colder, paths in Skyrim's frozen north (given that he expresses a dislike of extreme cold), and if he wanted to avoid the road that bridges Whiterun Hold and Eastmarch most directly; the latter road is worth avoiding due to the danger posed by bandits at Valtheim, but would Erandur be aware of their presence ahead of time?

Alternatively, if he started from Ivarstead, he may have wanted to avoid the Pale Pass by heading east and curving north, rather than traveling directly west. That would potentially bring him past or through Shor's Stone. However, if he started his journey from any further west than Ivarstead, or any further north than Windhelm, then there's no sensible path that would take him down through Shor's Stone.

As such, I think the most plausible timeline for Erandur's life is as follows:

* He's born in Cyrodiil and indoctrinated into a Vaermina cult.
* He travels or is brought to The Pale during his child or teenage years, and spends most of his life there.
* At some point during his early adulthood, he leaves The Pale to go to Solitude. He doesn't specifically renounce Vaermina worship, but he may be looking for another way of life. Unfortunately, he fails to fully conceal his affiliations and is shunned from the Bards College, returning to The Pale.
* He flees Nightcaller Temple and wanders Tamriel aimlessly for a while, eventually turning to Mara worship. Years pass.
  * His travels bring him as far west as the Reach. If he ever travels to the Rift, he consciously avoids Riften and the Temple of Mara out of shame.
* At some point, either of the following occurs:
  * He crosses into Morrowind, perhaps only briefly, before eventually making the decision to return to Dawnstar. He returns to Skyrim via the road near Riften, but continues to avoid Riften and the Temple of Mara, circumnavigating it and crossing through Shor's Stone. His route of travel takes him northward, then westward.
  * His travels bring him near or directly to Shor's Stone, and there, he makes the decision to return to Dawnstar. Thus he doesn't have to go far out of his way to meet Syljga at Shor's Stone.
* He returns to Dawnstar. The populace doesn't recognize him; no one has any actual dialogue about him save for the Jarl, who describes him as having been "sent" by Mara to help, so we can conclude that he doesn't live in Dawnstar proper and hasn't lived there for a long time, if ever. (Nonetheless, people have learned his name because he's the guy investigating their current plight.)

Thus he is not a resident of Dawnstar per se, but he is known by name to some of the townspeople.
