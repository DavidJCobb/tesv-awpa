
# Markarth

Markarth is not built *around* a mountain. The game's reduced scale makes the precise operational definition of "mountain" unclear, but per lore sources, the entirety of Markarth is built into the *side* of a mountain, and the elevated terrain at the city's center is a "crag."[^alleia] The crag divides the city into two regions: Riverside, to the south, and Dryside, to the north.[^alleia]

Two rivers run from west to east through Markarth, joining at the city's southeast wall. Riverside is named for the larger southern river; ergo "Riverside" as a term would include, at a minimum, every location from the Hall of the Dead backdoor and The Hag's Cure, down to Cidhna Mine and its smelter, and the warrens across from them. The smelter is the most notable location in Riverside, to the point that Alleia[^alleia] refers to the area as "the city's smelter district" while suggesting that industrial pollution has affected the water quality.

Although a smaller river runs through the city's northern half, most of the structures and paths on that side consist of elevated walkways built into a cliff face; thus the name Dryside. This is explicitly the upperclass area of the city.

The central crag doesn't appear to have a proper-noun name. It hosts the Temple of Dibella as well as a strategically located guard tower. A passageway cuts through the center of the crag, linking Riverside and Dryside; built into this passageway is a Shrine of Talos.

[^alleia]: Per in-game book *The City of Stone: A Sellsword's Guide to Markarth* by Amanda Alleia.

It is not clear which side of the city Understone Keep would be grouped with. The northern (Dryside) river runs through the keep's main structures, but sources like UESP consider Understone Keep a Riverside location.
